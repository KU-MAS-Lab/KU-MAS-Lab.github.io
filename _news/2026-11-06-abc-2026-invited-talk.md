Prof. Seongwook Choi was invited to deliver a talk at **The 12th Advanced Biophotonics Conference (ABC 2026)**, organized by the Optical Society of Korea and held at Hanwha Resort Haeundae in Busan from November 4 to 6, 2026.

He presented his research in the **Optical Tomography and Tissue Optics** session, sharing advances in photoacoustic imaging and acoustic-based biomedical imaging technologies.

The conference provided a valuable opportunity to exchange recent research developments, connect with researchers across the optics and biophotonics communities, and explore potential future collaborations.
