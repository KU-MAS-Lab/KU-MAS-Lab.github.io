# Research at MAS Lab

<p class="research-lead">MAS Lab develops multimodal acoustic systems that connect <strong>radiation, light, and ultrasound</strong> to quantitative imaging, disease monitoring, and therapeutic stimulation. Our central engineering question is: how can deposited energy or acoustic stimulation be converted into reliable, interpretable, and quantitative biomedical information?</p>

<p class="ko-summary" lang="ko">MAS Lab의 연구는 방사선·광·초음파를 공통된 음향 시스템 관점에서 연결합니다. 에너지 침적 또는 초음파 자극에서 시작해 신호 발생·검출, 재구성, 정량화, 생체 해석으로 이어지는 전체 파이프라인을 설계합니다. AI와 computational imaging은 각 연구축을 연결하고 정량성을 높이는 핵심 도구로 활용합니다.</p>

  <div class="research-band-copy">
    <span class="eyebrow">01 · RADIATION ACOUSTICS</span>
    <h2>Radiation-Induced Acoustic Imaging &amp; Dosimetry</h2>
    <p>We investigate acoustic waves generated by ionizing-radiation energy deposition. Our goals are to develop sensitive radiation-acoustic detection systems, reconstruct energy-deposition distributions, and establish quantitative links between acoustic signals and radiation dose. Topics include X-ray and radiotherapy-induced acoustics, real-time dosimetry, system synchronization, physics-based simulation, low-SNR reconstruction, and extensions toward emerging radiation sources.</p>
    <p class="research-ko" lang="ko"><strong>학생 연구 예시:</strong> 방사선-음향 신호 검출, X-ray/radiotherapy 실험, 선량-음향 신호 calibration, Monte Carlo/음향 시뮬레이션, 저 SNR 신호 향상, 3D 선량 영상화.</p>

</section>

    <span class="eyebrow">02 · ACOUSTIC BIOIMAGING</span>
    <h2>Photoacoustic &amp; Ultrasound Imaging</h2>
    <p>We develop photoacoustic and ultrasound platforms for structural, functional, and molecular imaging. Research spans 3D volumetric imaging, high-speed acquisition, spectral imaging, multimodal integration, image reconstruction, and system optimization for preclinical and translational applications.</p>
    <p class="research-ko" lang="ko"><strong>학생 연구 예시:</strong> 광음향/초음파 시스템 제작, 3D 재구성, 혈관 영상화, 산소포화도 분석, 고속 스캐닝, 멀티모달 영상, 영상 품질 및 시스템 최적화.</p>

    <span class="eyebrow">03 · QUANTITATIVE BIOMARKERS</span>
    <h2>Disease Monitoring &amp; Quantitative Biomarkers</h2>
    <p>We use longitudinal imaging to quantify biological change rather than only produce images. We study cerebrovascular and fluid dynamics, tumor microenvironments, oxygenation, pharmacokinetics, treatment response, and recovery, with the goal of deriving repeatable and interpretable imaging biomarkers.</p>
    <p class="research-ko" lang="ko"><strong>응용 방향:</strong> 뇌혈관·뇌척수액, 종양 미세환경, 약물·조영제 biodynamics, 질환 진행, 치료 반응을 반복 측정하고 vascular, oxygenation, molecular, fluid-dynamic 지표를 정량 바이오마커로 정의·검증합니다.</p>

</section>

  <div class="research-band-copy">
    <span class="eyebrow">04 · ACOUSTIC INTERVENTION</span>
    <h2>Ultrasound Stimulation &amp; Therapy</h2>
    <p>We engineer ultrasound exposure and monitoring strategies for neuromodulation, therapeutic stimulation, and rehabilitation-oriented applications. Rather than treating stimulation as a black box, we aim to systematically optimize frequency, pressure, pulse structure, and exposure conditions, while quantifying the resulting biological response with imaging and physiological measurements.</p>
    <p class="research-ko" lang="ko"><strong>학생 연구 예시:</strong> 초음파 주파수·강도·펄스 조건 최적화, 신경자극 및 therapeutic ultrasound 실험, 자극 전후 생체 반응 영상화, 재활 관련 기능 변화의 정량 평가, image-guided stimulation.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="representative-work" style="margin-top: 2.5rem;">
  <div class="section-head">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">CROSS-CUTTING METHODS</p>
      <h2>AI &amp; Computational Imaging</h2>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p>AI is not treated as a separate research destination in MAS Lab. Instead, model-based reconstruction, deep learning, physics-informed learning, segmentation, multimodal registration, uncertainty estimation, and GPU computing are used across all four research directions to improve sensitivity, speed, robustness, and quantitative reliability.</p>
  <p class="research-ko" lang="ko">AI는 독립적인 연구 목적이라기보다 방사선 유도 음향영상, 광음향·초음파 영상, 바이오마커 정량화, 초음파 자극 연구 전반을 고도화하는 공통 방법론으로 활용합니다. 물리 모델과 AI를 결합하여 저 SNR, 제한 시야, inverse problem, 자동 정량화 문제를 해결합니다.</p>
</section>
